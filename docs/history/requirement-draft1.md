# 🏥 Homeopathic Doctor Online Consultation Website – Requirements

## 1. Public Website (Patient-Facing)

### 1.1 Home Page

* Clear introduction of clinic and services.
* Quick links:  **Book Appointment** ,  **Contact** ,  **Services** .

### 1.2 About Us / About Me

* Doctor’s  **name & personal story** .
* Professional background (e.g., BHMS, years of practice,  *Dr. Zaid’s HomeoCare* ).

### 1.3 Services / Treatments

* List of conditions treated:
  * Chronic diseases
  * Lifestyle issues
  * Children’s health
  * Women’s health
  * Others
* Explanation of  **homeopathic approach** .

### 1.4 Book Appointment

* Appointment scheduling form.
* Options:
  * **Clinic Visit**
  * **Online Consultation** (video call / phone)
* Automated reminders (email/SMS).

### 1.5 Courses (Optional)

* Section for  **educational courses/trainings** .

### 1.6 Contact Us

* Contact options: **Phone, Email, WhatsApp button** (important in India).
* **Google Maps integration** for clinic location.
* Quick enquiry form.

### 1.7 Blog / Articles / Patient Education

* Section to post blogs, health tips, FAQs.
* Category/tag support.

## 2. Patient Case Record System (EMR – Electronic Medical Records)

### 2.1 Patient Information

* Name, Age, Gender.
* Contact details (phone/email).
* Occupation, lifestyle habits.
* Case ID / number (for tracking).
* Date of first consultation & follow-ups.

### 2.2 Chief Complaints

* Detailed entry (structured form):
  * Onset, duration, progression.
  * Location (where exactly).
  * Sensation (type of pain/feeling).
  * Modalities (better/worse conditions).
  * Concomitants (symptoms occurring together).
* Minimalist option:
  * Free-text notes for main complaint(s) with duration.

### 2.3 History of Present Illness

* Chronological development of complaints.
* Previous treatments (homeopathic, allopathic, ayurvedic, etc.).

### 2.4 Past & Family History

* Past illnesses (childhood diseases, major illnesses, injuries, surgeries, effects of anesthesia).
* Family hereditary conditions (DM, HTN, cancer, TB, etc.).
* Combined free-text section for simplicity in minimal mode.

### 2.5 Personal / General History

* Diet, appetite, thirst.
* Bowel habits.
* Sleep (position, dreams, disturbances).
* Addictions (tobacco, alcohol, etc.).
* Sexual history (if relevant).
* Thermal state (hot/cold patient).
* Food desires/aversions.
* Perspiration pattern.
* Menses (if female).
* Energy & stamina.

### 2.6 Mental & Emotional State

* Disposition: calm, irritable, anxious, suspicious, etc.
* Stress factors: family, job, relationships.
* Reactions to grief/anger/criticism.
* Fears, anxieties, obsessions.
* Memory & concentration.

### 2.7 Physical Examination

* Vitals: BP, pulse, temp, weight, height.
* Clinical findings (chest, abdomen, neuro, etc. if required).

### 2.8 Investigation Reports

* Upload & attach files (PDF, JPG, X-ray, USG, MRI, lab results).

### 2.9 Analysis & Evaluation

* Totality of symptoms (mark/tick important ones).
* Miasmatic analysis.
* Repertorization notes.

### 2.10 Prescription Record

* Remedy given (potency, dose, repetition, date).
* Follow-up remarks.
* Remedy response (better, no change, aggravation).

### 2.11 Follow-Up Notes

* Track progress of complaints.
* Record new symptoms.
* Modify totality as required.

## 3. Dashboard & Quick Features

* **Search Patient** : by name, ID, phone number.
* **Search by remedy given** (useful for audit/study).
* **Case Timeline View** (first visit → latest follow-up).
* **Symptom tags** (e.g., `<morning> headache`, `<after meals> pain`).
* **Voice-to-text support** for case recording.
* **Reminder system** (for follow-up dates).
* **Export case as PDF** (to share with patient).
* **Data backup & security** for confidentiality.
